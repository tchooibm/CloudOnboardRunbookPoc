# Billing and Payment Setup

Configure your billing preferences and payment methods for MyCloud.

## Add a Payment Method

### Step 1: Navigate to Billing

1. Click your profile icon in the top right
2. Select **Billing & Payments**
3. Click **Add Payment Method**

### Step 2: Enter Payment Details

**Credit Card:**

| Field | Format | Example |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Cardholder Name | Full name | John Smith |
| Card Number | 16 digits | 4111 1111 1111 1111 |
| Expiration | MM/YY | 12/25 |
| CVC | 3-4 digits | 123 |

### Step 3: Billing Address

Enter the billing address for your payment method:

- Street address
- City
- State/Province
- Postal code
- Country

### Step 4: Confirm

Review your details and click **Add Payment Method**.

## Set Up Billing Alerts

Protect yourself from unexpected charges:

1. Go to **Billing & Payments** → **Billing Alerts**
2. Click **Create Alert**
3. Set your alert threshold (e.g., $500/month)
4. Choose notification method (email)
5. Click **Save Alert**

## View Billing Information

### Current Charges

- Go to **Billing & Payments** → **Current Usage**
- View charges by service
- Export daily usage reports

### Monthly Invoices

1. Go to **Billing & Payments** → **Invoices**
2. Select the date range
3. Download or email invoices

### Usage Analytics

- **Cost by Service** - Breakdown of spending
- **Cost by Tag** - Spending by cost allocation tags
- **Cost by Resource** - Individual resource costs
- **Forecast** - Projected monthly costs

## Payment Methods

### Supported Methods

- ✅ Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
- ✅ Debit cards
- ✅ Bank transfers (business accounts)
- ✅ Purchase orders (enterprise customers)

### Set Default Payment Method

1. Go to **Billing & Payments** → **Payment Methods**
2. Click the three-dot menu next to a payment method
3. Select **Set as Default**

## Billing Cycle

- **Monthly billing** - Charged on the same day each month
- **Invoice date** - Usually within 5 days of month end
- **Due date** - 30 days from invoice date

## Free Tier Eligibility

New accounts may be eligible for free tier benefits:

- 100 GB free storage per month
- 100 hours of compute per month
- Free data transfer (up to 1 GB/month)
